Leather Body Bag

2026-09-21 Bymosaos
#Leathercraft #Make
  • Table of contents

I made this body bag after the body bag I received from my late wife became worn out.
The main part of the original bag had become badly worn, but the belt was still in good condition, so I reused the belt.
I happened to find some nice Japanese leather at Hands Shinjuku during a leather fair featuring leather from Japanese tanneries, so I used it for this bag.

I used black leather for the bottom part, with a separate piece for the transition.
The leather parts were hand-stitched.
The inner bag was sewn with a sewing machine.
